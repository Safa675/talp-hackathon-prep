# Audience attribute catalog

Your dossier describes your client's customers in two sentences. This catalog lists what a persona could have. Your dossier adds attributes for your sector.

Some of these matter a lot for your company and its crises. Many do not. Some matter only when one particular thing happens. Nobody will tell you which is which.

For each attribute you use, you decide:
- the values it can take;
- how they are spread across your personas, and why;
- its form: a fixed field, a line in a narrative, a memory, or something that changes over the ten weeks.

Say in `TEAM_DOC.md` what you used, what you left out, and why.

## A. Demographic
age · gender · state · city, suburb, small town or rural · household size · marital status · children and their ages · education · occupation · employment status (full-time, part-time, gig, self-employed, retired, student) · employer (private, federal, state or local government, self) · household income band · owns or rents their home · owns a car

## B. Life story
how long they have been a customer · a past bad experience with this company or a rival · a recent life event (new job, move, wedding, baby, retirement, layoff) · moved here from another state or country · has family far away · when and how they started doing things online · first in the family to go to college · served in the military

## C. Values and attitudes
what they value most (security, freedom, saving, status, family, convenience) · attitude to risk · trust in institutions · trusts brands or people they know · openness to new things · price consciousness · brand loyalty · environmental concern · sense of fairness · need for control

## D. Behavior
how often they use the service · how much they spend · preferred channel (app, website, phone, chat, in person) · uses deals and coupons · complaint history · writes reviews · social media use · also uses a rival · payment method (debit, credit, buy now pay later, cash) · time of day they use the service · plans ahead or decides at the last minute

## E. Emotions and reactions
baseline anxiety · how fast they get angry · patience when waiting · tendency to forgive · embarrassed to complain · fear of missing out · tolerance for uncertainty · feels treated unfairly · mood this week

## F. Social and information
where they hear news (family group chat, Facebook, X, Instagram, TikTok, Reddit, YouTube, podcasts, local TV news, cable news, coworkers, neighbors) · size of their circle · leads opinions or follows them · how easily others sway them · believes rumors or checks them · who they ask before deciding

## G. Money pressure
savings · credit card debt · student loans · credit score band · lives paycheck to paycheck · worried about prices · who pays (they, their parents, their employer)

## H. What they can do
complain in the app · call support · post a review (App Store, Google, Yelp, Trustpilot) · file a complaint with a regulator or the Better Business Bureau · post on social media · tell friends · dispute a charge with their card · use it less · pause · switch to a rival · wait and see · ask for a refund or a credit · go to small claims court · recommend the company · do nothing

## I. Lifestyle and personal details
zodiac sign · favorite color · favorite sports team · pet · favorite music · coffee order · morning or night person · iPhone or Android · height · favorite TV show · smoker · hobby · shoe size · left- or right-handed

## A note on fairness

Sensitive traits (race or ethnicity, religion, health, political views, immigration status) are not in this catalog. If you add one, say why your client's question needs it. Do not use any attribute to build a stereotype; the board will ask.
